ABOUT ME

Patricio Jiménez

My relationship with nature began long before the camera entered my life: it was a desire to understand it, to observe it closely, to recognize in every creature the same right to exist. Over time, that way of seeing grew deeper, and photography became the natural way to continue that silent dialogue. Today, I try to capture landscapes, natural scenes, and urban details that often go unnoticed, translating into images what my imagination sees in them. When I'm not behind the camera, you'll probably find me with my two little cats.

If my work has moved something in you, feel free to reach out to me.
